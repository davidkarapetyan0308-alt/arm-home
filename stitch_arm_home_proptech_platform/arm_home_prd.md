# Arm Home - Product Requirements Document (MVP)

## Vision
A modern, tech-forward real estate platform for the Armenian market, bringing Redfin-level UX, transparency, and data analytics to local users.

## Core Features (MVP)
1.  **Interactive Map:** Mapbox-based, showing property points with prices directly on the map. Focus on Yerevan neighborhoods.
2.  **Property Feed:** High-quality cards with key metrics (price, district, area, floor, renovation status).
3.  **Advanced Filters:** Price, rooms, area, district, new construction, mortgage eligibility, owner/agent.
4.  **AI Valuation:** Tagging listings as "Market Price", "Good Deal", or "Overpriced".
5.  **Agent Portal:** CRM, listing uploads, lead tracking, and chat.
6.  **Localization:** Full support for Armenian, Russian, and English.
7.  **Telegram Integration:** Alerts and easy sharing.

## Target Audience
-   Local buyers and renters in Yerevan.
-   The Armenian Diaspora looking for investment properties.
-   Real estate agencies and independent agents.

## Design Direction
-   Minimalist "Apple-style" UI.
-   Large, high-quality photography.
-   Smooth animations and transitions.
-   Light/Dark mode support.
-   Mobile-first approach (Flutter target).
